PDF Editor support

Get help with PDF files and Arabic or English text editing.

Email my.aliramadan@gmail.com.

Open and save a PDF

Choose Open PDF on the Files screen. The app imports a working copy, so your original file stays in its original location. Applied changes are saved in the app’s library. Choose Save copy to export through Files.

Edit Arabic and English

Choose Text, then tap text on the page to edit it. Tap an empty area to add text. Choose Apply to save the change, or Cancel to leave the page unchanged. Use your iPhone or iPad’s Arabic or English keyboard.

Text editing works with supported text-based PDFs. Scanned pages are images, and protected files may restrict editing. The mobile import limit is 128 MB.

Report a problem

Tell us your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and what you were doing. Include whether the issue concerns Arabic text, English text, saving or opening a file. You can describe the problem without sending your PDF.
