PDF Editor privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

Documents stay under your control

PDF Editor processes PDF files on your device. It does not upload documents to an app-operated server. The app does not include advertising, tracking or an app analytics service.

When you import a file, the app stores a working copy in its document library. Applied changes are saved there. Exporting a copy through Files sends the file to the location or service you choose. iCloud and other file providers handle that transfer under their own policies.

Storage and deletion

Working copies remain in the library until you remove them or uninstall the app. Removing a working copy does not remove the imported original or copies you exported elsewhere. Apple may include app documents in device backups depending on your settings; backup storage and deletion are controlled by Apple and your account settings.

Support

If you contact my.aliramadan@gmail.com, the support email provider handles your email address, message and any attachments you send. These messages are used to respond to your request. Contact the same address with questions about support correspondence or requests to delete it.

Apple services

App installation, TestFlight, App Store services, system diagnostics, device backups and cloud file providers are operated by Apple or the provider you choose. Their own privacy policies apply to those services.
